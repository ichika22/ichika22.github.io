A

New to Tradeview (new account)

01

Register for the member area

First, open the registration page from the dedicated link below. (Accounts not opened through this link can't use the EAs.)

Open the registration page (IB: 5233450)
Email addressAn email you check regularly (a verification email will arrive)
Password8+ characters mixing upper case, lower case, numbers and symbols (a format like Esora2026!)
Confirm passwordType the same password again

Click "Next", then tap the link in the verification email and log in to the member area.

💡 If a cookie banner appears, you can continue either with "Accept all" or by choosing settings under "Show details".
02

Name, country of residence and entity

Full nameIn Latin letters, exactly as on your ID (e.g. MARIA SANTOS)
Country of residenceYour country of residence
EntitySelect the Tradeview entity offered for your country. Japan residents see only Tradeview Financial Markets S.A.C. (Peru); residents of other countries may see a different entity or more than one
03

Tick the confirmation

"I acknowledge that I have not been asked to switch entities" simply confirms you are opening the account of your own free will. Tick it and click "Next".

04

Choose the account type

Account typeLive account (not demo)
Account holderIndividual (companies choose "Corporate")
Terms of useRead the disclaimer, tick the box and click "Next"
05

Identity verification notice

This screen lists the documents you'll need for verification later. Have them ready.

Photo ID (1 document)Passport / national ID card / driver's licence, etc.
Proof of address (1 document)Issued within the last 3 months: utility bill / bank or credit-card statement / government-issued residence certificate, etc.
Checkboxes(Required) consent to electronic identity verification. (Optional) marketing emails — fine to leave unticked
06

Enter your personal details

Phone numberPick your country code with the flag selector, then enter the number without the leading 0 (e.g. Philippines 0917 123 4567 → 9171234567)
Nationality / country of residenceAs on your documents
Date of birthPick from the calendar (yyyy-mm-dd)
State / provinceIn Latin letters (e.g. Metro Manila, Selangor, Bangkok)
CityIn Latin letters (e.g. Makati)
AddressYour street address in Latin letters, matching your proof-of-address document
Postal codeNumbers only, no spaces or hyphens if possible
U.S. citizen / resident for tax purposes?No (our service is not available to U.S. persons)
⚠️ The faint placeholder in the address field may read "Please enter your bank's address" — ignore it. Enter your own home address, not your bank's. It's just confusing wording on the form.
07

Choose the market and account settings

Select Foreign Exchange (Forex) as the market. On the next screen, choose exactly as in the table below (this is the most important step).

PlatformMT4 (MetaTrader 4) — not MT5
Account typeInnovative Liquidity Connector (ILC)
LeverageIf the form has a leverage field, choose 1:200. If there's no field, or the account opens at 1:100, you can change it to 1:200 in STEP 09
Account currencyUSD recommended
Additional commentsEnter "Please link to IB 5233450" (the number alone is also OK. Without it, the account can't be used with the EAs)

08

Identity verification (the quick way is fine)

ID documentChoose the document type from the dropdown (passport or driver's licence recommended)
Submission methodChoose quick verification (as fast as 2 minutes, recommended) to photograph your ID with your phone camera and finish immediately. If that doesn't work, "File upload" is fine too
💡 Photo tips: all four corners visible, text readable, no glare. Do that and it's almost always approved first time.

After submitting, you'll get an approval email in about 1–2 business days with your MT4 login details (account number, password, server name).

09

After opening: change leverage to 1:200

Right after opening, leverage is set to 1:100. Log in to the member area, open your account card, tap the ✏️ (pencil) next to "Leverage" → change it to "200".

⚠️ Many people forget this. At 1:100 the EA may not be able to place orders as designed. Always switch to 1:200 before depositing and going live.

Common stumbling points

The verification email doesn't arriveCheck your spam folder. Gmail is recommended
I forgot to enter 5233450 in the commentsJust DM us — we'll tell you how to fix it
My documents aren't approvedThe three usual causes: cut-off corners, blur, or a proof of address older than 3 months. A retake usually fixes it
I can't find ILCCheck whether "Innovative Liquidity Connector" appears in the account-type options. If not, DM us a screenshot
